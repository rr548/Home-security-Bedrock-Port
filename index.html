📱 Android Installation

  1. Download the .mcaddon file.
  2. Open your Downloads folder.
  3. Tap the downloaded file.
  4. Choose Minecraft.
  5. Wait for Minecraft to import it.
  6. Create or edit a Minecraft world.
  7. Activate the Behavior Pack.
  8. Activate the Resource Pack.
  9. Enter your world.
